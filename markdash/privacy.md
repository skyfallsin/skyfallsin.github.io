## Markdash privacy policy

_Last updated: October 18, 2026_

Markdash is a Markdown viewer and editor for macOS. It is built to stay out of
your way, and that includes staying out of your data.

### What Markdash collects

Nothing. Markdash does not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal
information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking of
any kind.

### Your files

Markdash only opens the files you choose to open, and everything it does with
them happens locally on your Mac. Your documents are never uploaded, copied to a
server, or sent anywhere. When you edit a block, only the bytes you changed are
written back to the file on your disk.

### Images from the web

If a document you open references an image by a web address, Markdash loads that
image from the web so it can be shown in the document — the same way a browser
would. This request goes directly to whatever server hosts the image and is the
only time Markdash reaches out to the network. Images stored on your Mac are
loaded locally and never leave your machine.

### Third parties

Markdash does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services,
and it does not share any data with anyone, because it has no data to share.

### Children's privacy

Markdash does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with
a new date above.
